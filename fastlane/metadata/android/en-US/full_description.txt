Zero-bloat system-wide equalizer built on Android's DynamicsProcessing engine.

Features:
* Real 5-band parametric DSP with 60fps morphing gain engine
* Limiter with automatic pre-gain compensation
* Bass boost + virtualizer
* Room correction via 3s log-sweep mic calibration
* On-device AI tuning — LiteRT-LM (Gemma), Gemini Nano, or bundled TFLite advisor; every response labeled with the backend that produced it
* Quick Settings tile, foreground mediaPlayback service (survives Android 14+ background limits)
* SQLite telemetry logbook with ASCII heatmap

No ads, no trackers, no accounts. Pure Kotlin, programmatic UI — no UI framework bloat. Free and open source; development supported via Ko-fi.

Requires: arm64 device, Android 8.0+.
